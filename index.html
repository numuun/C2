01 — Introduction

The
Opportunity

The rise of Agentic AI is driving the biggest transformation in the tech ecosystem since the launch of the internet.

  • PE/VC-backed SaaS and technology businesses are particularly exposed to AI disruption — significant risk of catastrophic losses for funds that get this wrong
  • Technology advances are moving faster than companies can adapt and talent can be developed
  • The majority of digital and technology investments have not delivered expected results
  • Leaders capable of closing the execution gap are genuinely scarce — hands-on agentic experience at scale is rare, demand structurally outpaces supply
  • Leadership mandates increasingly require proven experience in AI and agentic transformation
